# Duration and alignment evidence

The original media remains canonical. Timing reports describe evidence available to the CLI; they do not repair a presumed offset. [Media timing](../src/audio_cli/media/timing.py) extracts probe facts, [preparation](../src/audio_cli/pipeline/preparation.py) records the decoded audio used for inspection/enhancement, and [publication](../src/audio_cli/pipeline/publication.py) applies the enhancement duration policy. Encoding timestamp behavior is unchanged.

## Read the duration basis

| Public surface | Duration meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `inspect.source.duration_seconds`, enhancement `source`/`output`, and transcription capability `input` or plan `sample_output.source` | `duration_basis: probed_audio_stream` selects the first audio stream's finite, nonnegative duration; `probed_container` means that stream duration was unavailable and the container duration was used. These are metadata, not sample counts. |
| Inspection/enhancement `source.decoded_audio` and rendered `output.decoded_audio` | `duration_basis: decoded_pcm`; `sample_count / sample_rate_hz` from the audio actually decoded for this operation. The current enhancement decoder uses 48 kHz; its origin is `first_decoded_sample`. |
| A transcription run's `source.duration_seconds` | `duration_basis: canonical_decoded_pcm`; duration from the canonical mono 16 kHz PCM16 WAV frame count, rounded to six decimals. Word, segment, region, and range bounds use this zero-based decoded sample timeline, never an inferred container offset. |
| Render `measurements.after.duration_delta_ms` | Output minus original decoded audio duration, in milliseconds, with `duration_basis: decoded_pcm`. This is not the difference between the outer probed `duration_seconds` fields. |

If neither stream nor container supplies a usable duration, inspection omits the probed duration and its basis while retaining decoded evidence. Transcription planning refuses unavailable duration metadata; the enhancement command also requires it for its existing adjustment validation. Unavailable timing values remain absent, including unknown audio starts. A probed zero is retained as zero. Older v1 transcript sources without `duration_basis` remain readable and are not backfilled. Export still requires identical source dictionaries when merging, so legacy and newly annotated sources are not silently treated as identical.

## Read stream origins without claiming sync

`source.timing` and `output.timing` have `basis: probed_timestamps`. An available `container` object contains `start_seconds` and `duration_seconds`. `audio[]` and `video[]` list streams in probe order, identified by `stream_index`; the first audio stream is the one decoded. Each stream retains available `start_seconds`, `duration_seconds`, integer `start_pts`/`duration_ts`, and rational `time_base`. `initial_padding` and `trailing_padding` are copied only when the probe supplies them, in samples; they are not inferred from codec names or duration differences. Absent stream categories are omitted.

Each video stream with a known start and a known primary-audio start additionally carries `primary_audio_start_minus_video_start_seconds`. Positive values mean the probed audio start is later than that video's probed start. Comparing source/output values exposes a metadata-origin change, not measured lip sync, content delay, drift, or a justified timestamp correction. Stream headers do not exhaust edit lists, packet skip/discard information, codec priming, or padding. The CLI makes no content-to-container mapping from these fields. Transcription capabilities expose the same timing mechanics through the declared media facade; plans and run bounds do not add those starts to timestamps.

## What the render gate validates

`timeline_preserved: true` means exactly that the absolute difference between original and encoded-output decoded audio durations is at most **50 ms**, inclusive. The [gate](../src/audio_cli/pipeline/timing.py) compares rational sample counts at their actual rates before rounding; the displayed delta is rounded to three decimals. It does not validate content alignment, video duration, per-frame timing, relative A/V alignment, missing/duplicated words, or sample identity. Same-length shifted audio can pass. A longer or shorter decoded output outside the tolerance is refused before publication.

The accompanying `timeline_verification` states `scope: decoded_audio_duration_only`, `tolerance_ms: 50`, and `status: pass`. Both `content_alignment` and `av_sync` explicitly report `status: abstained`, `reason: not_measured`, including audio-only exports and matching stream starts. Dry runs report `status: not_run` and omit `timeline_preserved`; predicted measurements cannot validate a file that was not encoded. In older reports without `timeline_verification`, the boolean checked only the difference between probed durations, and dry runs could carry an unearned true value. Those reports are not decoded-duration or sync proof.

## Regression evidence and its limits

[CLI timing regressions](../tests/audio_cli/test_cli_timing_evidence.py) generate a 2.137-second, mono 48 kHz fixture with three windowed chirps near the beginning, middle, and end. The cases are AAC/MP4 with a nonzero audio origin, MP3 encoder delay/padding, and AAC/Matroska with different audio/video origins. They invoke public `inspect`, `transcribe plan`, and `enhance`, compare durable JSON, preserve the original hash, and check decoded landmark correlation within one sample after rendering. A same-duration 10 ms shifted negative control must fail that alignment bound. For video fixtures, decoded frame hashes must match, while the reports must expose changed stream origins and abstain on A/V sync. Synthetic tests replace VAD only; the AAC/MP4 case exercises program loudness and limiting, and the others skip treatments to isolate codec/publication behavior. They run no transcription model. A shortened-output encoder double must also be rejected before publication. These checks do not establish alignment quality for arbitrary speech, all codecs, or all applied DSP treatments.

The original issue's raw report at `model_tests/benchmark_runs/e2e-agent-retest-2026-09-05-195207/02-demo-enhancement/demo-enhanced.report.json` records probed durations 112.405333/112.405 seconds and audio starts 0.006/0.0 seconds, without video timing. The source for those numbers is the raw report, not a summary. It cannot establish a 6 ms content shift or exact sync. New evidence is additive; the original raw artifacts are not rewritten.
