## Root cause and recovery

The actual [strict aligner request](../../tests/artifacts/acceptance/2026-09-06-boundary-recovery/d-qwen/diagnostics/strict/audio-transcribe-xx267ln3/004-aligner-4sfkptka/request.json), [response](../../tests/artifacts/acceptance/2026-09-06-boundary-recovery/d-qwen/diagnostics/strict/audio-transcribe-xx267ln3/004-aligner-4sfkptka/result.json) and [canonical rejection](../../tests/artifacts/acceptance/2026-09-06-boundary-recovery/d-qwen/d2.strict.canonical.json) identify one rejected unit among 31 returned units and 238 returned words. `turn_25` / `seg_27` has input bounds 96.570437–97.996625 seconds. Its returned word spans 96.570–98.010 seconds: the start overrun is 0.437 ms, the end overrun is **13.375 ms**, and the effective allowance is **0.501 ms**. The adapter rejects that unit's word stream while retaining its ASR text and speaker. This is localized alignment containment failure, not a failed whole transcript or absent ASR text.

The pinned runner multiplies predicted timestamp classes by `timestamp_segment_time`; the recorded model configuration sets that value to 80 ms. The [runner/config identities and exact source excerpt](../../model_tests/benchmark/results/2026-09-06-qwen-alignment-boundary.json) support a coarse timestamp-grid explanation. They do not establish the true acoustic endpoint. The configured clipping policy accepts an estimate under an explicit limit; it is not a measured acoustic correction.

The authorized full-source Qwen retry used the same wants (`diarization,overlapped_speech,word_timestamps`) with a **20 ms** limit. All 31 raw returned units, their languages and all 238 raw words were identical between the two separately executed attempts. The [clipped canonical](../../tests/artifacts/acceptance/2026-09-06-boundary-recovery/d-qwen/d3.clipped.canonical.json) publishes all 238 words and records exactly one correction, linked to `turn_25`, `seg_27`, `w_182`, word index 0: original `[96.57,98.01]`, applied `[96.570437,97.996625]`, both measured overruns and the 20 ms cap. Previously published word text and bounds, segment text, speaker labels, turns and overlaps are unchanged. Inserting the recovered word renumbers 55 subsequent word IDs. Five unrelated withholding scopes remain; some abstention IDs also renumber after the alignment rejection disappears.

The receipt exposes requested outcomes plus exact conditional alignment rejections/corrections. A timed-export refusal exposes the measured rejection and remedies that distinguish never-requested timing from already-abstained timing. An agent can inspect the reason, choose an explicit limit or discover a supported alternative, then inspect a new result. The CLI does not automatically raise limits or select models. Invalid bounds, ordering and text reconciliation remain strict; an entirely invalid or collapsed positive word is not rescued by a larger cap. FireRed's recorded alternative run uses native word timing; the actual plan has no aligner role.

The subtitle defect was independent of the boundary cap. Three supplied words (`seg_10/w_94`, `seg_31/w_224`, `seg_31/w_225`) fell into cue candidates whose endpoints were equal after millisecond quantization. The old exporter dropped those candidates with a nonblocking warning. The new Qwen cue 16 retains `w_94` through `w_100` at 42.374–45.414 seconds; cue 43 retains `w_224` through `w_230` at 126.810–131.130 seconds. The corrected exporter combines such candidates with adjacent candidates within the same segment and speaker context, preserves every mapped word, and derives each cue's endpoints from the first/last supplied word. If the whole segment cannot form a positive interval, it refuses publication atomically. It never invents a word duration.
